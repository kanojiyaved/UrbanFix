# Business Requirements Document (BRD) - UrbanFix

## 1. Project Overview
UrbanFix is a comprehensive infrastructure and garbage management system designed to streamline the process of reporting, assigning, and resolving road damage (like potholes) and garbage accumulation.

## 2. Business Objectives
- Provide an accessible platform for citizens to report potholes and garbage accumulation.
- Enable government bodies to efficiently manage and assign repair and cleaning tasks.
- Facilitate contractors in tracking their resolution workflow.
- Improve overall road safety and infrastructure maintenance response times.

## 3. Stakeholders
- **Citizens:** Report potholes or garbage and view status.
- **Government Officials:** Review reports and assign contractors.
- **Contractors:** Receive assignments, execute repairs, and update status.
- **System Administrators:** Manage user accounts and platform configurations.

## 4. Scope
The scope includes a web-based platform with three main modules:
- Citizen Reporting Portal
- Government Assignment Dashboard
- Contractor Resolution Workflow

## 5. High-Level Requirements
- User authentication and role-based access.
- Image upload, issue type selection (Pothole/Garbage), and geolocation integration for issue reporting.
- Dashboard for task management and status tracking.
- Interactive map interface to visualize issue locations.
